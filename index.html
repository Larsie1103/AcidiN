    Trick Guide

    Welcome to Acidin!

    Choose your username. It will float above your skater.

    Choose your language:

    Basic controls:

    Move with W / Up, S / Down, A / Left, and D / Right.

    Push: press Space three times.

    Ollie: hold Shift + W.

    Click the game to capture the mouse. While airborne, flick right for a Kickflip or left for a Heelflip.

    Scroll down for a BS Shuvit or up for an FS Shuvit. Scroll farther for a 360.

    Near a rail, press Q for a 50-50 or E + Q for a Boardslide. Press E in the air for an Impossible.

    L opens Levels, T opens the Shop, and I opens Settings. Find trick combinations in the Trick Guide.
